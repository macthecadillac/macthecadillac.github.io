## About me

I graduated from the University of California, San Diego, with a PhD in Physics
specializing in physical oceanography in late 2025. My research interests are
centered on geophysical fluid dynamics, nonlinear systems, and machine learning.
My PhD work involved analytical and numerical studies of submesoscale ocean
dynamics in the mid-latitudes. Beyond physical oceanography, I have a background
in condensed matter theory and was a classically trained pianist prior to my
scientific career.

I am a co-maintainer of [FastVideo](https://github.com/hao-ai-lab/FastVideo), a
unified inference and post-training framework that aims to accelerate video
generation. My Rust package [atree](https://github.com/macthecadillac/atree) is
used by [C2PA](https://c2pa.org/) and has been downloaded over 11.2 million
times as of October 2026.

The background image is a visualization of SQG Kolmogorov flow using <a
class="external" href="https://dedalus-project.org">Dedalus</a> and <a
class="external" href="https://matplotlib.org">matplotlib</a>.
